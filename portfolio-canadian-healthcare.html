Canadian Healthcare Analytics Platform

An end-to-end healthcare analytics platform built on 64,000+ patient records and 16 years of real Canadian government data from the Canadian Institute for Health Information (CIHI). The project covers the complete analytics pipeline: Python ETL ingestion, SQL Server star schema (9 tables), a 5-page Power BI dashboard, three machine learning models, and a live deployed Streamlit prediction app.

The Power BI dashboard includes an Executive Summary with KPI cards and monthly admission trends, an ER Performance page with a time-of-day vs day-of-week wait time heatmap and CTAS benchmark analysis, and a Provincial Wait Times page showing 16 years of surgical wait trends across all Canadian provinces — with the COVID-19 spike clearly visible in 2020 across every province.

The ML Insights page showcases three models: a readmission risk classifier (Logistic Regression, XGBoost, Random Forest, SMOTE) with SHAP explainability, an XGBoost ER wait time regressor (R²=0.934), and a Prophet time series forecast predicting hip and knee replacement wait times through 2027. Key findings include Nova Scotia improving wait times by 38% since 2008, Quebec deteriorating by 172%, and urgency level dominating ER wait time predictions with a mean absolute SHAP value of 47.3.
