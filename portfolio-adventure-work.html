Adventure Works Dashboard

Built a two-page interactive Power BI report on four years of Adventure Works transactional data. The report centers on profit and delivers deep insight across time trends, product performance, customer demographics, and geographic distribution — enabling fast, data-driven decisions.

Dashboard 1 — Time Analysis

Answers when the business performs best — tracking profit and revenue across years, quarters, months, week types, and individual weekdays.

01
KPI Cards vs. Previous Year

Side-by-side Advanced Card visuals comparing COGS, Revenue, Quantity, Profit, Profit Margin, and Transaction count against the prior year — variance arrows make trends instantly obvious.

02
Yearly Performance (Above Average)

Column chart spotlighting years where Revenue, Profit, and Transactions exceeded the 4-year average — visually separates standout periods from underperforming ones.

03
Monthly Profit Trend

Line chart covering all 48 months — reveals seasonality, growth momentum, and any anomalies across the full data window.

04
Profit by Week Type

Donut chart splitting profit between weekday and weekend sales, answering whether peak revenue is driven by business-day or leisure shopping.

05
Quarterly Profit Analysis

Advanced Cards comparing Q1–Q4 profit with custom quarter icons for quick visual navigation — useful for planning seasonal campaigns.

06
Profit by Weekday

Bar chart breaking profit down by day of the week (Mon–Sun), helping pinpoint peak and low-performing trading days for staffing and promotion decisions.

Dashboard 2 — Detail Analysis

Answers what and who drives profit — dissecting products, customer demographics, geography, and pricing strategy.

01
Top 5 Products by Profit

Treemap with percentage contribution — identifies the five most profitable products alongside an "Others" segment, enabling clear product prioritisation.

02
Top 5 Customers by Profit

Highlights the five highest-value customers with their % share of total profit — supports VIP loyalty strategy and targeted relationship management.

03
Profit by Gender

Pie chart comparing male vs. female customer profit contribution — informs gender-targeted marketing and product placement decisions.

04
Profit by Product Color

Bar chart ranking product colors by profit with best-seller highlights — guides inventory purchasing and visual merchandising strategy.

05
Profit by Pricing Type

Scatter Plot examining the preferance of purchasing.

06
Country-wise Profit (Azure Map)

Interactive Azure Map visual plotting geographic profit distribution — enables regional sales targeting, expansion planning, and territory benchmarking.

07
Profit by Age Group

Table showing which customer age demographics drive the most profit — identifies core buyer personas and untapped demographic segments.
